Mine $GLaDOS

Your spare PC can mine $GLaDOS tonight, and now real-world assets too. Boot the miner, give it your wallet, pick what you want to be paid in, and walk away: $GLaDOS, NVIDIA, SpaceX, a basket of chip makers, or gold, silver and oil. It lands straight in your wallet.

Start mining

  1. Hold 1,000,000 $GLaDOS in a wallet on Robinhood Chain. The contract address is on the token page.
  2. Download glados-1.3.10-miner.iso and check it against SHA256SUMS.
  3. Run it in QEMU, or boot a real PC from a USB stick.
  4. Give it your wallet. Type it at first boot, or put it in GLADOS/MINER.TXT on the stick. It remembers it from then on.
  5. Let it run. Your $GLaDOS shows up on its own.
First boot. Type your wallet and press Enter. That's the whole setup.
Or skip the typing. Put your address on the worker line of GLADOS/MINER.TXT on the stick, and the PC starts mining the moment it boots.

Any x86-64 PC with UEFI works, roughly 2011 and newer. Turn Secure Boot off and plug in Ethernet, or share your phone's connection over USB.

Choose what you're paid in

Type a code on the mining screen and press Enter. The miner restarts and mines for what you picked. Or set the reward line in GLADOS/MINER.TXT, like reward metals. Either way it's remembered on that PC.

How you get paid

  • Everyone gets the same. Each payout is split equally between every eligible wallet, and each share is paid in what that wallet chose.
  • Who is eligible: wallets holding at least 1,000,000 $GLaDOS that did at least a quarter of the typical miner's work in that round. Miss a round and your work carries over to the next.
  • How often: as soon as the pot is big enough that fees stay under 10% of it. More miners means more frequent payouts.
  • Straight to your wallet on Robinhood Chain. There is nothing to claim.
All it ever needs is your address. The miner never asks for a key or a signature.
